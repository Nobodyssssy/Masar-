# Publication and LinkedIn correction

Global historical eligibility counts are not a recommendation score. Strict personal rules and missing descriptions can leave many records unverified. Broad search remains available without those rules.

Search and Review share the latest publication window. Choosing a relative period clears custom dates; contradictory requests are rejected. A one-day view uses rolling 24 hours for timestamps and today's date for date-only advertisements. A freshly collected old publication cannot qualify as new. Full history is explicitly unfiltered.

LinkedIn cards are enriched before final keyword relevance checks. The warehouse query recognizes explicit German/French warehouse terms; quoted exact phrases stay literal, and language filters are independent. A live Swiss query retained 20 relevant vacancies from 30 inspected. Authored fixtures use synthetic identifiers and are never imported as real vacancies.

Indeed, Emploi en Suisse, 100000jobs and Job-Room remain manual official links.
